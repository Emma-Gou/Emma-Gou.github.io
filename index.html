Professional Profile

An elementary educator with more than 11 years of teaching experience in the United States and China.

My work focuses on strong literacy foundations, thoughtful differentiation, and helping students become confident, independent learners. I have taught Grades 3 and 4 in U.S. public, dual-language, and international school settings, with experience in English language arts, guided reading, inquiry-based learning, and assessment-informed planning.

I use explicit instruction, small-group teaching, academic-language scaffolds, and meaningful learning experiences to support students with varied strengths and language needs. I also enjoy collaborating with colleagues, developing practical instructional resources, and using student evidence to guide next steps.
